🧴 Suggested Daily Routine
Golden rule: thinnest to thickest
Apply watery serums before creams, wait ~30–60 seconds between layers so each absorbs, and always finish the morning with SPF — azelaic acid and caffeine both perform better on UV-protected skin.

✨ Product Cards
Caffeine Solution 5% + EGCG Eye Serum
The Ordinary · Under-eye 🔗 Product
What it targets: Under-eye puffiness, dark circles, and tired-looking eyes.
  • Caffeine 5%: de-puffs and visibly brightens the under-eye
  • EGCG (green-tea antioxidant): protects and soothes the delicate eye skin
  • Clinically visible improvement in dark circles & puffiness in ~3 weeks
How it works & target Caffeine is a vasoconstrictor and mild diuretic — it tightens the dilated blood vessels behind bluish dark circles and drains the fluid that causes puffiness. EGCG (epigallocatechin gallatyl glucoside) is a potent green-tea polyphenol that neutralizes free radicals and calms inflammation in the thin peri-orbital skin.
Vasoconstriction Fluid drainage Antioxidant (EGCG)
How to apply: pea-sized amount, dab along the orbital bone with your ring finger and pat in gently — after cleansing/toner, before moisturizer.
AM PM
Pro tip: keep it in the fridge — the cooling de-puffs even more, ideal in the morning or after a late night. Give it ~3 weeks of daily use for dark circles.
Azelaic Acid 10 + Hyaluron Treatment
Anua · Redness / texture 🔗 Product
What it targets: Redness, breakouts, uneven texture, and post-acne marks.
  • 10% Azelaic acid: calms redness, clears and prevents breakouts, evens tone
  • 3 types hyaluronic acid: offset dryness so the acid stays comfortable
  • Calming complex (cica, aloe, green tea, blue tansy): soothes irritation
How it works & target Azelaic acid is antibacterial against C. acnes, anti-inflammatory (quiets the redness of acne/rosacea), normalizes pore-lining cell turnover (unclogs pores), and inhibits tyrosinase → fading hyperpigmentation and post-acne marks. It is gentle enough for sensitive skin, unlike most exfoliating acids.
Anti-C. acnes Anti-inflammatory Tyrosinase inhibition Pore normalization
How to apply: 1–2 drops (up to 5) between toner and moisturizer. Start 2–3×/week and build to daily as tolerated.
PM preferred AM ok + SPF
Caution: a mild tingle is normal — ease in slowly. If you use it in the morning, sunscreen (SPF 30+) is non-negotiable. Do not pile it on top of other strong actives while breaking it in.
PDRN Hyaluronic Acid Capsule 100 Repair / Hydrate
Anua · Barrier & glow 🔗 Product
What it targets: Deep hydration, plumping, barrier repair, and a dewy glow.
  • PDRN (salmon DNA fragments): stimulates repair, elasticity, smoother texture
  • 11 types hyaluronic acid: multi-depth hydration, plumps fine lines
  • Hydrolyzed collagen: reinforces the skin barrier and firmness
How it works & target PDRN (polydeoxyribonucleotide) activates fibroblasts and the adenosine A2A pathway → driving DNA synthesis, tissue repair and micro-circulation, which improves texture and elasticity. The multi-weight hyaluronic acid pulls water into every skin layer while collagen shores up the barrier — together giving that plump "glass-skin" finish.
Fibroblast activation Barrier repair Multi-depth hydration Elasticity
How to apply: a few drops patted in after treatments, before moisturizer — ideal right after azelaic to re-hydrate.
AM PM
Pro tip: at night you can layer 2–3 coats as a mini "sleeping mask" for extra plump by morning. This is your barrier-repair workhorse — it keeps skin calm and hydrated alongside the azelaic.
📋 Tips & Cautions
  • SPF is the multiplier. Azelaic, caffeine and PDRN all deliver better results on UV-protected skin — SPF 30+ every morning, no exceptions.
  • Introduce one active at a time. Azelaic is the only strong active here — give it 1–2 weeks before pushing to daily. Caffeine and PDRN are gentle and can be daily from day one.
  • Layering: watery serums first, cream last; wait ~30–60 seconds between layers.
  • Patch test a new serum on the jaw for a couple of days before full-face use.
  • Consistency > intensity. Most results show at the 3–4 week mark with steady daily use — stick with it.
